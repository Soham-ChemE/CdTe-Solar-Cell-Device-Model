# Calibration target: Kujovic et al. 2024, 21.4% As-doped CdSeTe/CdTe with resistive ZnO

L. Kujovic et al., "Achieving 21.4% Efficient CdSeTe/CdTe Solar Cells Using Highly Resistive Intrinsic ZnO Buffer Layers", Adv. Funct. Mater. 34, 2312528 (2024), doi 10.1002/adfm.202312528. Licence CC BY 4.0; open copy at Loughborough repository record 24902478.

- Authors: Loughborough, Swansea and First Solar (W. Zhang, C. Lee, T. Nagle, D. Lu, G. Xiong).
- The absorber and back contact were deposited by First Solar.
- The paper states that its data are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request.
- The PDF and its extracted text are kept locally and are not redistributed.

## Device

FTO (SnO2:F, ~1e20 cm⁻³) / intrinsic ZnO 50 nm (Hall carrier density below 1e12 cm⁻³, deposited at room temperature) / As-doped CdSeTe/CdTe absorber / ZnTe + metal back contact.

- The absorber was activated in CdCl2 vapour at 400–500 °C.
- Cell area 0.445 cm².

## SCAPS parameters stated by the authors (Methods)

These are the authors' modelling choices, not measurements.

- Linear doping grading from 3e16 cm⁻³ (CdTe) to 1e16 cm⁻³ (CdSeTe).
- Linear band-gap grading from 1.50 to 1.41 eV.
- Absorber lifetime 10 ns.
- Acceptor-type interface defects at ZnO/CdSeTe, 1e9 cm⁻², corresponding to S = 1e4 cm/s.
- ZnO/CdSeTe conduction-band offset +0.1 eV (spike). The authors say they still have to measure it. XPS on ZnO/CdSeTe elsewhere (Pandey et al. 2021) gives a −0.10 eV cliff.
- FTO carrier density 1e20 cm⁻³, ZnO 1e11 cm⁻³.
