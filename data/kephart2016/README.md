# Kephart et al. 2016: UPS band alignment of oxide front layers on CdTe

J. M. Kephart, J. W. McCamy, Z. Ma, A. Ganjoo, F. M. Alamgir, W. S. Sampath, "Band alignment of front contact layers for high-efficiency CdTe solar cells", Sol. Energy Mater. Sol. Cells (2016). Public-access copy: OSTI 1534330. The PDF and its extracted text are kept locally and are not redistributed.

What it gives Option A:

- **UPS electron affinities** for SnO2-based alloys, ZnO and 11 wt% MgZnO high-resistance transparent (HRT) layers (Table 1).
- **Electron affinity predicts device voltage.** For cells without CdS, the HRT electron affinity strongly predicts Voc and efficiency. The trend weakens with 50 nm oxygenated CdS and weakens further with sublimated CdS (Fig. 7).
- **SnO2-based oxides** range from flat to a 0.9 eV conduction-band cliff against CdTe.
- **SCAPS baseline used by the authors:**
  - absorber lifetimes 2 ns
  - acceptor-like midgap interface defects at oxide/CdTe, 2e12 cm⁻²
  - interface recombination velocity 1e7 cm/s
  - a J-V kink appears when the oxide affinity drops to 4.0 eV
- **Oxygenated sputtered CdS** has a free-electron density of about 1e12 cm⁻³ after a 500 °C anneal (Hall).

Use: priors for oxide electron affinity and front-interface defect density in the buffer model. The Table 1 numbers are still to be transcribed from the PDF figure or table.
